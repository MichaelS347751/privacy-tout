Privacy Policy for TOUT — Dice and Card Game

Effective date: July 15, 2026

TOUT ("the App") is a dice-and-card game with optional advertising. This privacy policy explains what data the App accesses, stores, and how advertising partners handle data.

Data Collection

TOUT does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data itself.

The App has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, and no server-side data storage.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following information entirely on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device:

DataPurpose
House rules settingsRemembers your preferred game rules between sessions
Game session stateEnables you to save and resume a game in progress
Game event logRecords dice rolls and game events; optionally exported as a .txt file

No network transmission of this local data occurs.

Advertising

The App displays ads via Google AdMob (Google's advertising service). AdMob may collect and process data for the purpose of serving personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General location (not precise GPS)
  • Ad interactions (clicks, impressions)

GDPR Consent (EU/EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent dialog on first launch. You can choose to accept personalized ads or receive non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time via the Privacy Settings option in the App's Settings screen.

Ad Placements

  • Main Menu — Banner ad at the top of the screen
  • Game Over Screen — Banner ad at the top
  • Win Screen — Full-screen interstitial ad (shown 5 seconds after winning)

How to Opt Out

  • On Android: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • In the App: Go to Settings → Privacy → Ad Consent to manage your choices.
